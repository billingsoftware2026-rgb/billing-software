Password Reset & WhatsApp Share

If a store owner forgets their password:
1. Go to Manage Stores and open the store profile.
2. Check the Saved Password Record field and click Share ID & Password on WhatsApp.
3. If they changed their password earlier, click Send Reset Email and ask them to check their Spam/Promotions folder as well.

Data Loss / Missing Bills

If a store reports missing jobs:
1. Open the store profile.
2. Go to the Backup tab.
3. Upload their JSON backup in Restore Data to merge all bills, customers, EMI, and stock automatically.
